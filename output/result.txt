Question: total spend by department

SQL:
SELECT d.department_name AS department, SUM(t.amount) AS total_spend FROM transactions t JOIN departments d ON t.department_id = d.id GROUP BY d.department_name ORDER BY total_spend DESC

Explanation:
Adds up all transaction amounts for each department and lists departments from highest to lowest total spend.
